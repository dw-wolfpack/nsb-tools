FAQ

Last updated: April 4th, 2026

NSB Tools is a small set of fast tools that run in your browser. The free tier does not require signup. Optional NSB Pro adds higher limits, presets, and CSV workflows on select calculators after Stripe checkout and email verification. If you want workflows and checklists to go with the tools, start at the AI Hub.

Do I need to sign up?

No. All tools run in your browser. No account required.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Inputs and outputs stay on your device. We use localStorage only for preferences and recently used tools.

How does sharing work?

Most tools support share links that encode your inputs in the URL so you can reopen the same view later. If a page has a Share button, it is safe to copy and send.

Do you store my inputs?

No. NSB Tools does not store your inputs on a server. Some tools may save lightweight preferences locally in your browser (like last used category or recently used tools).

Can I use this for client work?

Yes. Outputs are drafts and numbers to help you move faster. Always sanity check anything high-stakes before you ship or send it.

Where should I start?

  • Browse categories to find tools by use case.
  • AI Toolkit for prompts and “best next tool” picks.
  • Glossary if you want quick definitions for common terms.

What is NSB Pro?

NSB Pro is live. It raises or removes daily generator limits while your access is active, adds named presets (with export and import), and enables full CSV import and export on the loan / debt payoff and burn rate / runway calculators. Subscribe monthly or buy lifetime access in the upgrade modal; you verify with the same email after Stripe so Pro unlocks in this browser.

Open the Pro page for the short version and account tools.

How does the free tier work?

You can use every tool without creating an account. To keep the site sustainable, free use may be capped with a daily generation limit. Share links, calculators, and copy-first workflows behave the same; you simply get more runway on Pro.

How do I unlock Pro after paying?

Finish checkout in Stripe, then complete the email verification step using the address you paid with (links also appear on /pro/ and in your receipt email when configured). Pro unlock is stored in local storage on the device where you verify so your inputs stay client-side.

Billing and refunds

Charges and subscription management run through Stripe. Use the links in your Stripe receipt or customer portal to update payment methods or cancel a subscription. NSB Tools does not store your card number.
