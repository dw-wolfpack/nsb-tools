About NSB Tools

NSB Tools is a collection of fast, client-side tools for creators, writers, and professionals. Everything runs in your browser. No backend, no signup, no tracking.

Last updated: April 4th, 2026

What it is

These are simple tools for getting unstuck and moving faster: writing drafts, quick calculators, and checklists that turn messy inputs into usable output.

Most tools are built to be copy-first. Generate, skim, tweak, paste, ship.

Who it is for

  • Creators who need hooks, scripts, captions, and content ideas
  • Job seekers who want cleaner resume bullets, STAR stories, and outreach drafts
  • Founders and operators who want fast math: break-even, runway, pricing, ROI

How it works

Tools run client-side. Inputs stay on your device. Some pages generate shareable links so you can save scenarios, compare options, or send a friend the same view.

Free vs NSB Pro

The whole catalog stays usable without an account. NSB Pro is optional: it increases generator headroom while your subscription or lifetime purchase is active, adds named presets you can export and move between browsers, and unlocks CSV import and export on the loan payoff and burn runway calculators. Upgrades are billed through Stripe; you confirm with email so Pro turns on locally without us hosting your workbook data.

See the Pro page or open Upgrade from the site header.

Trust

No signup requirement for the free tier, no server-side storage of your tool inputs, and no ads-driven tracking model: just local execution and honest limits. If something looks off in an output, treat it as a first draft: you are still the editor.
