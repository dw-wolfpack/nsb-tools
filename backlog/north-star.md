# North star

## Personal milestone

**Target:** about **$3,000 / month post-tax and post-platform fees** from this product (and closely attached offers), sustained enough to trust the line.

That number is a **confidence milestone**, not a vanity metric: it proves distribution + offer + delivery hang together.

## Bridge the gap (you must model this)

Post-tax/post-fees implies:

- Stripe (or other) fees come **before** “post-fees.”
- Income tax + self-employment (if applicable) come **after** gross but before “post-tax.”

**Action:** In a spreadsheet, row in: gross revenue → payment fees → pre-tax net → estimated tax burden → post-tax net. Update quarterly with real numbers.

Rough planning anchors (not tax advice):

- If effective all-in tax + SE load is ~25–40%, gross might need to land **~1.4–2x** the post-tax target depending on your situation.
- Add buffer for refunds/chargebacks if you scale checkout.

## Ladder (qualitative)

1. **Proof of utility:** Returning visitors, saves, shares, support surface calm (few “broken” reports).
2. **Proof of willingness to pay:** First paid conversions, low refund noise, clear reason people pay (save time / unlock workflow).
3. **Proof of repeatability:** You can ship improvements without heroics; metrics move when you ship.
4. **Proof of scale:** Organic + owned channels bring steady growth; economics improve with volume or price, not hope.

When the ladder is solid, **$3k post-tax/fees** is an output of the system, not a wish.

## Non-goals (for now)

- Chasing every channel at once.
- Perfection on v1 of analytics or entitlements before **one** crisp upgrade story exists.
