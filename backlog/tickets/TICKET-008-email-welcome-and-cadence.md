# TICKET-008: Email — welcome + repeatable cadence

**Status:** ready  
**Band:** P1  
**Workstream:** Growth  
**Agent hint:** Growth agent (copy + ESP)

## Problem

Mailchimp capture without **welcome** and **cadence** leaves list cold. Email is cheap leverage for return visits and Pro consideration.

## Outcome

- Welcome automation or first send: who you are, best tool for ICP, Pro in one sentence.
- Cadence: e.g. **monthly** minimum with one clear CTA per email.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Welcome path live (or manual process documented if automation blocked).
- [ ] 3-email outline bank (topics tied to tools/workflows).
- [ ] Unsubscribe + privacy alignment (what Mailchimp stores).

## Metrics

- Leading: open/click (if available); list growth net of churn.
- Lagging: attributed sessions from email (UTM); Pro conversions from email cohort (rough).
