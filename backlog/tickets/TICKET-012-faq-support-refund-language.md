# TICKET-012: FAQ / support / refund language

**Status:** ready  
**Band:** P1  
**Workstream:** Product / Trust  
**Agent hint:** PM + legal common sense

## Problem

Ambiguous policies create **chargebacks** and support thrash. Paid users need “what if it doesn’t work” and “how do I cancel.”

## Outcome

FAQ or Terms sections: refunds (if any), how to cancel subscription, how Pro unlock works, data handling (inputs), contact path. Tone: honest, not corporate mush.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] FAQ updated; cross-linked from `/pro/` footer area.
- [ ] Stripe Customer Portal or “email to cancel” documented if portal not used.
- [ ] Privacy cross-check after analytics (TICKET-001).

## Metrics

- Lagging: dispute rate; repeat questions in inbox (informal count).
