# TICKET-019: Competitive substitute map (one page)

**Status:** ready  
**Band:** P2  
**Workstream:** Product  
**Agent hint:** PM agent

## Problem

You need to know what users **do instead** (Excel, ChatGPT, agency, incumbent SaaS). Pricing and copy improve when substitutes are explicit.

## Outcome

One-pager: top 5 substitutes, their price/time tradeoff, your **unfair advantages** (speed, privacy posture, specific calculators). Internal use; optional public “compare” later.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Substitutes listed with honest strengths (no strawmen).
- [ ] One line “why we still win” per substitute.
- [ ] Feeds TICKET-005 Pro narrative refresh.

## Metrics

- Qualitative: faster decisions on what to build next.
